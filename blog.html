Blog and Health Tips

Short articles for healthier habits.

These starter articles demonstrate the blog section planned in the concept note. More articles can later be stored and retrieved through the database.

01

Hydration matters

Water helps the body regulate temperature, maintain energy, and recover after exercise. Beginners should keep water nearby during workouts.

02

Start with consistency

A short routine done regularly is more useful than a hard routine abandoned after two days. FitLife Hub encourages small progress.

03

Rest is part of training

Rest days help muscles recover and reduce injury risk. A balanced plan should include activity, nutrition, and sleep.
