About the Project

Built to reduce fitness information overload.

Many beginners cannot afford trainers or nutritionists and often find online health information confusing. FitLife Hub centralizes simple guidance in one student-built web application.

Mission

To help students, young adults, and fitness beginners develop healthier routines through structured workouts, meal ideas, and progress tracking.

  • Free and beginner-friendly guidance
  • Responsive design for phones and computers
  • Interactive tools that support daily use
