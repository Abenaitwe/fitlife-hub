MUST BIT1203 Web Design and Development

Fitness and nutrition made simple for beginners.

FitLife Hub is a responsive web system that brings workout routines, meal suggestions, health tips, and progress tracking into one easy platform for students and young adults.

Project Aim

To support healthier lifestyles through accessible fitness guidance and nutrition tracking.

The system is designed for beginners who need a structured starting point. It offers clear routines, simple meal ideas, interactive tools, and a dashboard that helps users monitor progress over time.
